DEFERRED / ACCEPTED RISK
------------------------
Late-arriving data is intentionally deferred because its expected frequency is
currently very low. The accepted limitation is that a record arriving with an
updated_at older than the committed watermark will not be picked up by the
normal incremental run. Use an explicit backfill when such a case is detected.
Revisit this decision when source latency becomes measurable or operationally
significant; likely controls are a lookback window, ingestion timestamp, CDC,
and late-data monitoring.
